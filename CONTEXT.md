# Personal Portfolio

Domain language for the portfolio site: what visitors browse and download.

## Language

**Experience**:
The on-site record of Roles, Education, and Certifications.
_Avoid_: resume page, CV

**Role**:
A job held at an employer, including the work done there.
_Avoid_: Experience, position

**Education**:
A program of study at a school. An Experience can include more than one.
_Avoid_: Role, degree-as-job

**Certification**:
A named qualification granted by an issuing organization.
_Avoid_: Role, Education
